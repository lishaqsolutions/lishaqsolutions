Modernizing a Government Authority Website

This project involved rebuilding and modernizing a government authority website together with a secure administrative management portal. The objective was to improve content management, streamline administrative operations, strengthen security, and provide a better experience for citizens accessing information online.

The legacy system had become difficult to maintain, lacked proper administrative workflows, and was not optimized for modern devices. We developed a custom Laravel-based solution that addressed all these challenges while ensuring scalability and future growth.
