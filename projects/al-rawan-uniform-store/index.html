Al Rawan Uniform Store

Custom uniform ordering platform for schools, hospitals, corporate offices, industrial companies, security firms, and medical organizations.

Core PHP MySQL Bootstrap AJAX JavaScript

Project Overview

Al Rawan specializes in manufacturing and supplying uniforms for organizations across multiple industries. The company required a professional online platform where clients could view products, submit inquiries, request quotations, and place bulk orders.

We developed a comprehensive uniform ordering platform that serves B2B clients across schools, hospitals, corporate offices, factories, security firms, and government organizations. The platform streamlines the entire quotation and ordering process.

📊

No Online Lead Generation

Limited Product Visibility

👤

Difficult Customer Management

Customer information and history were scattered across different systems.

📩

No Centralized Inquiries
