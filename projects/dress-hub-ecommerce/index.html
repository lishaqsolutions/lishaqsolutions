E-commerce Case Study

Dress Hub E-commerce Website

A complete online clothing store developed with customer accounts, product management, shopping cart, order processing, and administrative controls.

Core PHP MySQL Bootstrap JavaScript AJAX

Project Overview

Dress Hub required an online platform where customers could browse products, place orders, manage accounts, and complete purchases efficiently. The objective was to create a scalable e-commerce platform with a simple shopping experience and complete business management tools.

We developed a custom e-commerce solution that enabled Dress Hub to establish an online sales channel, streamline order processing, and provide customers with a seamless shopping experience.

Business Challenges

🛒

No Online Ordering Process

Customers had no way to browse and order products online, limiting sales potential.

📦

Manual Inventory Tracking

Inventory was tracked manually, leading to stockouts and overordering issues.

📋

Difficult Order Management

Orders were managed through manual processes, causing delays and errors.

👤

No Customer Account System

Customers had no way to track their orders or manage their profiles.

📊

Poor Reporting Visibility

No system to track sales performance, popular products, or customer trends.

📈

Growth Limitations

Manual processes were limiting the business's ability to scale and grow.
