Barioo ERP System Upgrade

A complete redesign and modernization of an existing ERP platform, improving usability, performance, navigation and operational efficiency while preserving critical business workflows.

Laravel Bootstrap 5 AJAX jQuery PHP MySQL ERP

Project Overview

The client was operating an ERP platform that had become difficult to use due to an outdated interface, inconsistent workflows and aging frontend components. Although the core business logic remained valuable, the system required modernization to improve employee productivity and support future growth.

The goal was to upgrade the ERP platform with a modern Bootstrap 5 interface, improved user experience and enhanced responsiveness without disrupting daily operations. The modernization preserved years of business logic while delivering a significantly improved user experience.
