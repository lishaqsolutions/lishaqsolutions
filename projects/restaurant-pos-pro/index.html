Case Study

RestaurantPOS Pro

Multi-branch restaurant POS system developed using Laravel and Bootstrap.

Laravel Bootstrap MySQL jQuery AJAX

Project Overview

RestaurantPOS Pro is a complete restaurant management solution designed to handle billing, kitchen operations, inventory, sales reporting and staff management. The system streamlines daily operations for restaurant chains and single-branch establishments alike, providing real-time visibility into sales, inventory, and employee performance.

This project was developed for a growing restaurant chain with 5 branches across the city. The client needed a unified system that could handle their complex operations while being easy for staff to use.

Client Challenges

📋

Manual Billing Process

Staff was using paper receipts and manual calculations, leading to errors and slow service.

📦

No Inventory Tracking

Stock levels were tracked manually, resulting in frequent stockouts and overordering.

📊

No Sales Analytics

The client had no visibility into sales trends, popular items, or peak hours.

🏢

Multiple Branches Difficult to Manage

Each branch operated independently with no centralized reporting or control.

Our Solution

🖥️

Real-Time POS

Fast, intuitive point-of-sale interface with touch support and quick item selection.

👨‍🍳

Kitchen Display System

Orders sent directly to kitchen screens with real-time status updates.

📈

Sales Reports

Comprehensive reports on sales, revenue, taxes, and item performance.

📦

Inventory Management

Automatic stock updates with low-stock alerts and supplier management.

👤

Role Based Access

Different access levels for managers, cashiers, and kitchen staff.
