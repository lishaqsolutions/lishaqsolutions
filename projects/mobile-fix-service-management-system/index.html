Mobile Fix Service Management System

A complete service management platform developed for mobile repair businesses to track repairs, customers, technicians, invoices, payments, and service history from one centralized dashboard.

Laravel PHP MySQL Bootstrap 5 AJAX jQuery

Managing Mobile Repair Operations Efficiently

Mobile Fix needed a centralized platform to manage repair jobs, customers, technicians, payments, invoices, and service records. The existing process relied on manual registers and spreadsheets, making it difficult to track repairs and customer history.

We developed a complete service management platform that tracks every repair from customer intake to final delivery. The system includes technician assignment, repair status updates, invoice generation, and detailed reporting.

Challenges

📋

Lost Repair Records

Repair records were kept manually and frequently lost, making it difficult to track customer history.

📄

Manual Invoice Creation

Invoices were created manually, leading to errors and delays in billing customers.

👤

No Technician Tracking

No system to track which technician was assigned to which repair or their workload.

📊

Poor Customer History Management

Customer service history was scattered across multiple registers and hard to retrieve.

⏰

Delayed Service Updates

Customers had no way to check the status of their repairs, leading to frustration.

📈

Difficult Reporting Process

Generating business reports was time-consuming and often inaccurate.
