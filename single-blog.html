The Laravel Performance Checklist I Use on Every Project

👤 Muhammad Ishaq 📅 Apr 12, 2026

Most teams ship features fast and worry about performance later. That's fine — until "later" becomes "the site is down and customers are calling." The good news: the fixes are usually mechanical once you know where to look.

The problem

You inherit (or built) a Laravel app. Pages take 3+ seconds. The dashboard times out at month-end. Someone whispers the word "rewrite." Don't. Most apps just need a checklist applied with discipline.

A practical approach

Start with measurement. Don't optimize what you haven't measured. Add timing, log slow queries, and watch the actual numbers — not your gut.

// Log slow queries in Laravel
DB::listen(function ($query) {
    if ($query->time > 200) {
        Log::warning('Slow query', [
            'sql'  => $query->sql,
            'time' => $query->time,
        ]);
    }
});

What this gets you

Within a day you'll have a clear list of the worst offenders. Fix them one by one. Most apps see 50–80% latency improvements just from adding the right indexes and removing N+1 queries.
